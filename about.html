About Us

Travotel Hotel
Enjoy a Luxury Experience

The Travotel Suites, Nagpur is a top rated hotel ideally located on Wardha Road, just minutes from the Airport and Mihan. Its prime location makes it a top choice for business travelers, corporate guests, transit passengers, and families, offering unmatched convenience and connectivity to Nagpur’s commercial hubs.

The hotel features spacious, well-designed rooms with contemporary interiors, plush bedding, dedicated workspaces, high-speed Wi-Fi, and modern amenities to ensure a relaxing and productive stay. Whether you’re on a short business trip, attending meetings at MIHAN, or enjoying a longer stay, the rooms provide both comfort and functionality.

A standout feature of the property is Mocha Sky Restaurant, the rooftop dining venue offering panoramic city views. Guests can enjoy a wide selection of Indian and international cuisines, along with refreshing beverages, making it the perfect spot to unwind after a busy day of travel or work.

The Travotel Suites also offers essential facilities such as 24-hour front desk and room service, airport transfers on request, free parking, conference and banquet facilities, laundry services, and doctor-on-call, making it an ideal all-round choice for business and leisure travelers.

With its strategic location near Nagpur Airport and MIHAN, spacious rooms, modern amenities, and vibrant rooftop dining at Mocha Sky, The Travotel Suites is one of the most preferred hotels in Nagpur for both business and leisure stays.
